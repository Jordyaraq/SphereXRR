Abstract—This paper presents the design of an interactive
virtual reality system in which the movement of a spherical object
generates real-time three-dimensional spatial audio. Physical
variables such as position, direction, and velocity are mapped to
digital signal processing parameters. The system implements bin-
aural spatialization based on Head-Related Transfer Functions
and includes a trajectory recording mechanism that reconstructs
the generated sound sequence during inactivity. The project
demonstrates an engineering-based multimedia system integrat-
ing real-time rendering, procedural audio, and performance
evaluation metrics.
Index Terms—Virtual Reality, 3D Audio, Binaural Spatializa-
